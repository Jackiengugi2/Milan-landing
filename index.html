Here are the honest answers to what parents ask us most.

Isn’t play just a break from learning?

Not here. Every play station, game and project has a learning goal behind it, so children build real skills while they’re having fun.

Will my child keep up academically?

Play sits inside a clear structure of circle time, routines and guided lessons. Learners who are ready are stretched further, and every child is supported to grow.

My child learns differently. Will they fit in?

Inclusion is part of how we teach. Lessons are adapted so every learner can take part fully, without ever being singled out.

Parents often ask

Questions, answered

What does “integrated play” mean?

Play isn’t separate from lessons. It’s how lessons happen, especially in the early years. A sand table teaches measuring, a building corner teaches shapes and teamwork, and a pretend shop teaches numbers and language.

What is experiential learning?

Learners work through real tasks and projects themselves instead of just being told the answer. They remember more, think more independently and grow in confidence.

What are the school fees?

Our fee structure varies by class and is shared directly with parents. Tell us the class your child is joining in the form, or call/WhatsApp +254 115 044201.

Do you offer transport?

Share your estate in Thogoto, Kinoo, Dagoretti or nearby, and we’ll confirm whether our transport covers your route, plus the charges.

Can I visit with my child?

Yes, please do! Choose a date in the form or call +254 115 044201. Your child is welcome to join a play station while you talk with our teachers.
